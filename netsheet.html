How the sheets compare

Key differences

Amounts marked + are credits to the seller. All other amounts are costs or payoffs paid from the seller's proceeds. Lines that are zero on every sheet are left off. This is an estimated netsheet and all figures are subject to change. Title and escrow fees are estimates, loan and lien payoffs are subject to the lender's payoff demand, and prorations are estimated from each sheet's closing date using 30 day months. Property taxes paid outside of closing are not shown. Your final figures will appear on the closing statement prepared by escrow.
